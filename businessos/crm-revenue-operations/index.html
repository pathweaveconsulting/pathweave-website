WHY CRM PROJECTS STALL

A CRM cannot fix an unclear sales process

Most CRM projects that stall do not fail on software. They fail because the system was configured before anyone agreed how the business actually sells.

You may recognise the signs:

Sales stages mean different things to different managers

Forecasts are rebuilt in spreadsheets every month

Reps update the CRM just before reviews — or not at all

Leads, deals and customer data sit across several tools

Leadership cannot see which deals are real and which are hope

A CRM was bought, launched, and quietly abandoned

We start with your sales rules, roles and the decisions leaders need to make — and only then design the system.

WHAT WE DO

CRM and revenue operations services

01

Sales process and pipeline design

Define pipeline stages with clear entry and exit criteria, ownership, handoffs between teams, and probability logic that reflects how your deals actually move.

02

CRM requirements and solution design

Translate the sales process into CRM design: users and roles, objects and fields, workflows, permissions, integrations and the reports each role needs.

03

Salesforce and Dynamics 365 implementation

Configuration oversight, data migration, workflow setup, testing and launch on Salesforce or Microsoft Dynamics 365. Where you already have an implementation partner, we act on your side — owning the requirements and holding the build to them.

04

Pipeline governance and forecasting

Weekly pipeline reviews, forecast categories, data-quality standards and action tracking that turn the CRM into a management rhythm rather than a reporting chore.

05

CRM adoption and sales enablement

Role-based training, manager coaching routines, usage standards and post-launch support so the system is used every day, not just at month-end.

06

Revenue operations metrics

Funnel conversion, sales velocity, pipeline coverage, forecast accuracy and activity quality — defined once and tracked consistently.
