BusinessOS · Sales Operations & RevOps

Build the operating backbone behind the revenue engine.

PathWeave helps leadership teams strengthen the planning, CRM discipline, pipeline governance, reporting and management routines that let sales teams operate with clearer priorities and better visibility.

Discuss Sales OperationsExplore CRM & Revenue Operations

Where the operating model breaks

  • Targets and territories are set once a year but rarely recalibrated
  • Pipeline definitions differ by team or manager
  • Forecasts are rebuilt manually outside the CRM
  • Leadership reports require reconciliation before they are trusted
  • Salespeople see CRM as administration rather than a working tool
  • Ownership of data, process and review cadence is unclear

Role of Sales Operations

Make the sales organisation easier to plan, run and inspect.

Sales Operations sits between strategy and day-to-day sales execution. It turns commercial priorities into targets, territories, process rules, CRM discipline, reports and review routines that managers can actually use.

RevOps broadens the lens when lead management, handoffs, pipeline and reporting need coordination across functions. PathWeave applies the model pragmatically rather than forcing a specific organisation chart.

Operating backbone

What Sales Operations and RevOps should own

01

Planning and targets

Annual and quarterly planning, target logic, assumptions and capacity translated into a manageable operating plan.

02

Territories and coverage

Account allocation, territory potential, coverage models and role clarity so effort is matched to opportunity.

03

Pipeline governance

Stage definitions, mandatory evidence, close-date rules, ageing, next steps and review standards that improve pipeline quality.

04

CRM operations

Data definitions, workflows, user roles, quality controls and adoption routines that keep CRM useful after launch.

05

Reporting and forecasting

Trusted dashboards, forecast categories, review packs and management information built on consistent business definitions.

06

Performance governance

KPIs, incentives, action tracking and review cadence that connect operating data with management decisions.

Decision rights

Separate process ownership from system administration.

Business ownerOwns sales-process definitions, review logic and the decisions the data must support.
Sales Operations / RevOpsOwns operating standards, reporting, planning, pipeline governance and continuous improvement.
CRM / technology teamOwns configuration, integrations, technical controls and platform administration.
Sales managersOwn execution, coaching, data discipline and action follow-through in the field.
