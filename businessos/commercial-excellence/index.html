BusinessOS · Commercial Excellence

Improve the quality of revenue, not just the quantity.

Commercial Excellence connects pricing, margin, incentives, channel economics, sales governance and management information so revenue growth creates stronger business performance rather than hidden leakage.

Discuss Commercial ExcellenceExplore the combined practice

Typical signals

  • Revenue grows but margin quality deteriorates
  • Discounting varies by person, region or customer
  • Incentives drive volume but not the outcomes the business values
  • Channel partners grow sales without improving profitability or reach
  • Pricing exceptions are frequent and hard to govern
  • Commercial reviews use multiple versions of the same number

The commercial system

Commercial Excellence manages the trade-offs behind profitable growth.

Sales Excellence focuses on how the sales organisation executes. Commercial Excellence extends the lens to the economics and governance around that execution: pricing, discounts, margin, mix, incentives, channel performance and commercial decision rights.

The objective is not tighter control for its own sake. It is better commercial decisions made with common definitions, visible trade-offs and clear authority.

Core levers

Where Commercial Excellence creates discipline

01

Pricing and discount governance

Price architecture, approval thresholds, exception logic and discount visibility so commercial flexibility does not become uncontrolled leakage.

02

Margin and revenue quality

Contribution, mix, collections, rebates and net-realisation measures that show whether growth is financially healthy.

03

Incentive economics

Plans that reward the right balance of revenue, margin, collections, product mix, new customers or strategic priorities.

04

Channel economics

Distributor, dealer and direct-sales economics, coverage productivity and trade investment viewed as a system rather than isolated schemes.

05

Commercial governance

Decision rights, approval forums, exception management and review cadence for commercial choices that cross Sales, Finance, Marketing and Supply Chain.

06

Commercial analytics

Management information that links sales activity to revenue, margin, mix and channel performance using consistent definitions.
