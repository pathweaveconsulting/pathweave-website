BusinessOS · Business, management & technology transformation

Business and management consulting for growing SMEs

Strengthen the systems behind growth. PathWeave works with founders and leadership teams to connect strategy with sales, operations, data and technology, then turn priorities into practical ways of working.

Start with a Sales & CRM Health Check Discuss your growth priorities

You may recognise the signs

  • Sales results depend on a few people rather than a repeatable process
  • Priorities change every month and teams are unsure what matters most
  • Leaders wait days for reports that are assembled by hand
  • Processes differ by branch, team or manager, and nobody owns the end-to-end flow
  • Technology was bought, but teams still work around it in spreadsheets and messages
  • The founder is still the final decision point for too many things

How the pieces fit

Growth is an operating system, not a strategy deck.

Growth rarely stalls because the opportunity disappears. It stalls because the way the business runs has not kept up. Strategy, sales, processes, data and technology cannot work as disconnected pieces.

BusinessOS treats them as one system: direction at the top, a commercial engine that runs every week, and foundations that keep it working as you grow.
