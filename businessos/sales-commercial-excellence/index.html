Why sales growth stalls

When sales growth depends on effort rather than a system

Many growing businesses hit a ceiling where adding salespeople no longer adds proportionate revenue. The reason is rarely effort. It's the system around the sales team.

You may recognise the signs:

A few people deliver most of the numbers, and results fall when they leave

Targets are set top-down each year with little link to territory potential or capacity

Every salesperson sells differently, and managers can't say which approach works

Incentives reward volume while margin, collections or new-customer growth suffer

Sales reviews discuss numbers but rarely end with clear actions and owners

New hires take too long to become productive

We start with how your business actually wins and loses deals, and then design the plan, process, incentives and management rhythm around it.

What we do

Sales Excellence and Commercial Excellence services

01

Sales planning and target setting

Annual and quarterly sales plans, target setting based on territory potential and team capacity, and the assumptions behind the number made explicit.

02

Territory, coverage and sales structure

Territory and account allocation, coverage models by customer segment, sales roles and span of control, so effort goes where the revenue potential is.

03

Sales process and playbooks

A defined way of selling — stages, key activities, qualification and handoffs — captured in practical playbooks for each role. When the process needs to live in a CRM, our CRM and revenue operations services take it into the system.

04

Incentive and compensation design

Sales incentive plans that reward the outcomes that matter, whether revenue, margin, collections, new customers or product mix, with clear rules, simple calculations and a cost the business can afford.

05

Sales performance management

KPIs by role, performance reviews, coaching routines and structured management of under-performance, so managers lead on evidence rather than impressions.

06

Sales governance and review rhythm

Weekly, monthly and quarterly sales reviews with clear agendas, decisions and action tracking. For the dashboards behind them, see our sales dashboards and BI consulting.
