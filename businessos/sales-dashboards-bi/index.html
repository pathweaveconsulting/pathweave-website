WHY REPORTING FAILS

More reports do not always create better visibility

Most businesses do not lack reports. They lack one version of the numbers that everyone trusts and acts on.

You may recognise the signs:

Someone spends days every month consolidating spreadsheets into a sales review

“Revenue”, “pipeline” and “conversion” mean different things in different reports

Reports arrive after the decision they were meant to inform

Dashboards track activity counts that nobody acts on

Leaders see a problem in the numbers, but no one owns the follow-up

A BI tool was licensed, but the team still asks for the Excel version

We start with the decisions leaders need to make and the questions they ask every week — and only then decide what to measure and build.

WHAT WE DO

Sales dashboard and BI consulting services

01

Decision and KPI design

Start from the decisions and questions each leader faces. Define every metric once — its formula, owner, target, data source and review frequency — so that a number means the same thing in every meeting.

02

Data-source and quality assessment

Review CRM, ERP, finance and spreadsheet sources for gaps, duplicates, conflicting definitions and unclear ownership, and agree what has to be fixed before anything is built.

03

Dashboard information design

Design executive, manager and team views with the right level of detail for each role: drill-downs, filters, exceptions and the few numbers that should be visible at a glance.

04

Data model and dashboard development

Transformation logic, data model, dashboard build, testing and an agreed refresh process — delivered on Power BI or Tableau, whichever fits your data environment and existing licences.

05

Reporting governance

A metric dictionary, data ownership, refresh responsibilities and change control, so the dashboard stays accurate after launch rather than drifting within a quarter.

06

Decision rhythms and adoption

Weekly sales reviews, monthly business reviews and action tracking built around the dashboard, so it becomes how the business is run rather than another link in an email.

PLATFORMS

Technology and platform approach

The platform follows the data environment, not the other way round. We start with where your data lives today — CRM, ERP, accounting software or spreadsheets — and recommend the simplest setup that gives leaders reliable, timely numbers.

Our dashboard delivery experience is on Power BI and Tableau. Where you already use one of them, we build on it. Where you don't, we recommend the one that fits your data sources, users and budget.

You already have a BI tool We fix definitions, data and design before rebuilding anything.
You are choosing a BI tool We define reporting requirements first, then assess fit and cost.
Your data is mostly in spreadsheets We structure the data and the reporting routine before recommending new software.
