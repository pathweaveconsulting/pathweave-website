About PathWeave

Built from experience. Designed for what comes next.

PathWeave is a boutique management consulting firm, founded in April 2020. We bring business, people and technology together to help organisations move from where they are today to what they need to become.

Why PathWeave exists

Transformation is easier to talk about than to make real.

Organisations rarely struggle because they lack another idea. More often, the challenge is turning the right idea into a way of working that people can understand, adopt and sustain.

PathWeave was built around that reality. Our work connects strategy with operating models, people with processes, and technology with execution — so change becomes part of how the organisation works rather than another initiative running alongside it.

We work across business transformation, people transformation, skills and government advisory, bringing the right combination of experience and practical execution to each situation.

What we believe

Good transformation should leave the organisation stronger than it found it.

That means solving the immediate problem while building the capability, ownership and systems needed for what comes next.

Business first

Transformation has to connect to a meaningful business outcome — growth, efficiency, capability, resilience or scale.

Practical by design

We favour solutions that teams can understand, adopt and operate over approaches that add complexity.

Built to sustain

The goal is systems that keep working as the organisation grows and changes — not just a fix for today.

How we are different

Not slides. Systems your team can run.

Recommendation-only consulting

  • Ends with a report and a presentation
  • Starts from a standard framework
  • Ownership is handed over at the end
  • Success means the deliverable was accepted

How PathWeave works

  • Ends with something running — a process, a CRM, a review rhythm
  • Starts from a diagnosis of your organisation
  • Ownership is built with your team during the work
  • Success means it was adopted and measured
