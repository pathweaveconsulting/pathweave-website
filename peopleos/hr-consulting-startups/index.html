WHY STARTUPS NEED A DIFFERENT HR APPROACH

Build the right HR foundations at the right time.

Startups rarely need a large HR function from day one. What they need is the right people structure, processes and support for the stage they are in — and the ability to build on those foundations as the organisation grows.

In the early stages, founders often manage people matters themselves because the team is small and priorities change quickly. But as the organisation grows, that approach becomes harder to sustain. Hiring takes more time, manager capability becomes a priority, and people decisions increasingly affect business performance.

PathWeave helps founders make that transition, moving from founder-dependent people management to practical, scalable systems at the pace the startup actually needs.
