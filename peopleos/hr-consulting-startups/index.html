WHEN IT MAKES SENSE

The people systems that worked at ten employees may fail at fifty

In the early days, the founder is the HR function. That works — until the team grows faster than the habits behind it.

Every people decision still runs through a founder.

Offers, leave, pay and conflicts compete with product and fundraising for founder time.

Offers are inconsistent.

Similar roles have different titles, pay structures and terms, and people notice.

Onboarding is rushed.

New joiners get a laptop and a Slack invite, but no clear first 30 days.

Roles have blurred.

Early employees do a bit of everything, and nobody is sure who owns what.

Records are missing.

Appointment letters, policies and employee data are incomplete or scattered.

First-time managers are managing without support.

Goals, feedback and performance conversations happen differently in every team.

STAGE BY STAGE

Right-sized HR support for each startup stage

The stages describe where you are, not a fixed package.

STAGE 1

First hires and essential foundations

Employment documentation, a short set of core policies, a consistent joining process and basic employee records — the minimum you need to hire safely and consistently.

STAGE 2

Scaling teams and managers

Role clarity, a simple organisation map, manager routines, a first performance cycle and team communication practices as you add layers.

STAGE 3

Preparing the people function to scale

An HR operating model, HRMS and automation, people metrics and a decision on who owns HR next — an internal hire, fractional leadership or an outsourced model.

RIGHT-SIZED

What we will not overbuild

Startups get hurt by too little HR and by too much. We decide what to build based on three things:

HeadcountWhat your team size actually requires today and in the next 12 months.
RiskWhere missing documentation or inconsistent decisions create real legal, retention or reputation exposure.
Growth plansHiring pace, new locations, funding milestones or new leadership layers.

If a policy, process or tool doesn't serve one of these, we leave it out.

PathWeave provides HR consulting, not legal advice. Where a matter needs a legal opinion, we'll say so and work alongside your legal advisers.
