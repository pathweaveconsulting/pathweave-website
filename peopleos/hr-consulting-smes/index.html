When IT makes sense

When informal HR starts slowing the business

What worked at 20 people often stops working at 100. The signs are usually familiar.

People decisions still go through the founder.

Leave, pay, hiring and conflict all wait for one person.

Similar situations get different answers.

Without clear policies, decisions depend on who is asked.

Roles and reporting lines have blurred.

People are unsure who owns what, and work falls between teams.

Policies are missing, outdated or not followed.

Documents exist, but practice varies across teams.

Managers were promoted for performance, not people skills.

They need tools and routines for goals, feedback and difficult conversations.

Employee data is scattered.

Records sit across spreadsheets, email and paper, and basic reports take days.

Our approach

HR consulting designed for SME realities

SMEs don't need large-company bureaucracy. They need the right level of structure for the organisation they are becoming.

PathWeave helps SME owners assess where HR needs to improve, then strengthens the areas that matter most — in an order the business can absorb.

More structure where it helps. Introduce consistency where it improves clarity, accountability and the employee experience.
Less complexity where it doesn't. Keep HR practical and proportionate to the size and needs of the business.

What an SME HR engagement can deliver

Phase options: a focused diagnostic (priorities and roadmap only), a defined improvement project (one or two areas), or a phased programme across the full HR function.

  • HR diagnostic and priority roadmap
  • Organisation structure and role charters
  • Core policy framework
  • Employee lifecycle workflows
  • Performance-management system
  • Manager toolkits
  • Recruitment and onboarding process
  • HRMS requirements and implementation plan
Process

How the engagement works

Diagnose→Prioritise→Design→Implement→Enable
  1. 1. DiagnoseReview how HR works today across policies, roles, managers, data and systems.
  2. 2. PrioritiseAgree the few changes that will make the biggest difference first.
  3. 3. DesignBuild the policies, structures, processes and tools with your leadership team.
  4. 4. ImplementRoll out changes with managers and employees, in a sequence the business can absorb.
  5. 5. EnableHand over templates, routines and ownership so your team can run HR without us.
