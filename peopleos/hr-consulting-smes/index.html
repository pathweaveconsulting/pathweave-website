WHY SMEs NEED A DIFFERENT HR APPROACH

Move from informal HR practices to a stronger people function.

As businesses grow, the people practices that worked when the organisation was smaller can become harder to manage consistently. What worked smoothly at 20 people may not work at 100.

Hiring increases. Teams become larger. Managers take on greater responsibility. Employee expectations change. Policies need to be clearer. Performance needs more structure. HR information becomes harder to manage manually.

The answer isn't simply to add more HR processes. It is to build the right level of structure for the organisation you are becoming. PathWeave helps SME owners assess where HR needs to improve, strengthening the areas that matter most without building bureaucracy.

WHEN IT MAKES SENSE

When the business has outgrown how HR is being managed.

The need often becomes visible when the way you have been managing people no longer keeps pace with the business.

HR is becoming reactive.

The HR team or leadership spends most of its time responding to immediate issues rather than improving how the people function works.

Policies exist, but processes aren't consistent.

The organisation has rules and documentation, but execution and compliance vary wildly across teams.

The organisation has outgrown its structure.

Roles, reporting lines or responsibilities are no longer as clear as they need to be, creating bottlenecks.

People information is becoming difficult to manage.

Employee data, workflows and reporting increasingly depend on spreadsheets, manual work or disconnected systems.

Leadership is spending too much time on HR issues.

Owners and senior leaders are increasingly pulled into people matters that need clearer processes and ownership.

Managers are handling people differently.

Different teams have developed entirely different approaches to hiring, performance, communication or employee issues.
