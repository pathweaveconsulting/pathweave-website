PeopleOS · Global Capability Centres

Design the people operating model for a GCC that has to work across global and India teams.

A GCC needs more than hiring capacity. It needs clear role architecture, global-local decision rights, manager capability, employee-lifecycle processes, service ownership and people data that support both local execution and global integration. PathWeave focuses specifically on that people operating system.

Start with an HR Health CheckDiscuss your GCC people model

Where friction usually appears

  • Global and India HR responsibilities are not explicit
  • Hiring scales faster than role and manager architecture
  • Shared services, COEs and local HR overlap
  • Managers inherit teams without clear people responsibilities
  • Employee-lifecycle processes vary by function
  • People reporting exists but decision ownership is unclear

The operating question

What should be global, what should be local, and who owns the decisions between them?

That question sits at the centre of GCC people design. A global policy or COE can define standards, but India leaders and managers still need workable local ownership. Likewise, local HR can run delivery without becoming the default owner of every people decision.

PeopleOS makes those interfaces explicit, then connects them to workforce planning, organisation structure, talent acquisition, manager routines, HR operations and data.

Three stages

The people model changes as a GCC moves from establishment to scale

01

Establish

Define workforce plan, initial organisation structure, role architecture, hiring ownership, employee lifecycle, HR service model and manager responsibilities before headcount accelerates.

02

Scale

Add management layers, strengthen talent acquisition, formalise performance and career practices, improve people analytics and increase HR service capacity without creating unnecessary bureaucracy.

03

Strengthen

Clarify global-local ownership, improve COE/shared-service interfaces, refine role architecture, build leadership depth and improve governance where the original setup no longer matches the scale.

Global-local HR architecture

Make the interfaces explicit before they become escalation paths.

Global HR / COEsDefine enterprise standards, architecture, common frameworks and selected specialist expertise.
India HRTranslate standards into workable local processes, run local service delivery and surface where the operating model needs adaptation.
GCC leadershipOwn workforce choices, organisation decisions, manager expectations and people outcomes for the India operation.
ManagersOwn hiring decisions, onboarding quality, goals, feedback, performance and team-level people routines within the defined framework.
Shared servicesRun repeatable transactions and data processes with clear service boundaries, measures and escalation rules.

Six design areas

What the GCC people operating system needs to connect

01

Workforce planning

Translate capability, location and function plans into role families, hiring sequences and management capacity.

02

Organisation & role architecture

Clarify levels, spans, accountabilities, reporting relationships and interfaces with global teams.

03

Talent acquisition

Define role intake, sourcing ownership, assessment, interview governance and handoffs as hiring volume grows.

04

Manager capability

Equip managers to run goals, feedback, performance and employee decisions rather than escalating routine people work to HR.

05

HR operations & service delivery

Design employee-lifecycle workflows, local/global handoffs, service ownership and escalation across HR, shared services and COEs.

06

People data & governance

Define data ownership, reporting logic, governance forums and management questions before adding dashboards or automation.
