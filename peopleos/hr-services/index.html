WHY HR NEEDS TO EVOLVE

HR requirements change as your business changes.

What works when you have 15 people may not work when you have 75. Processes become harder to manage, managers need more support, and decisions that were once informal start needing structure.

And when the organisation is expanding, restructuring or transforming, the people challenges change again.

You don't always need another full-time HR hire. Sometimes you need the right expertise, for the right problem, at the right time.

That's where PathWeave's PeopleOS approach comes in.
