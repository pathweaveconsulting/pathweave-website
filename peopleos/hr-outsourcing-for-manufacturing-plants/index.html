PeopleOS

HR Outsourcing for Manufacturing Plants

When the shift roster, attendance register and contractor bill tell three different stories, payroll week becomes a reconciliation exercise. PeopleOS helps your plant agree one process and runs the HR work you assign to us.

Discuss your plant’s HR needs

What we can run for your plant

Daily attendance and shift exceptions

Track absences, joiners and gaps in skill coverage. Give shift supervisors the exceptions they need to resolve before deployment. Production retains responsibility for staffing each station.

Weekly hiring and contractor checks

Follow up on operator vacancies, joining dates and induction. Compare contractor attendance and overtime entries with supervisor approvals, then route discrepancies to a named owner.

Monthly payroll inputs and workforce review

Prepare reconciled inputs and a list of unresolved items before payroll cut-off. Review overtime, absenteeism, vacancies and early attrition with the plant head. Finance authorises payments.

Choose the support your team needs

A plant with an HR executive may need help with one recurring activity or a senior leader to support workforce decisions. A multi-shift plant may need a wider operating scope and scheduled on-site coverage.

Start directly with HR Outsourcing for recurring delivery. Choose a focused HR Health Check to trace a problem such as repeated payroll exceptions. For senior decision support, explore Fractional HR.

Scope the engagement

Tell us your plant location, number of shifts, approximate employee and contractor headcount, current HR team and the first process you want covered. We use these details to agree responsibilities, visit frequency, payroll deadlines, reporting and fees.
