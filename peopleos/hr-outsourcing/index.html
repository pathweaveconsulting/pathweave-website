When IT makes sense

When HR work is recurring but ownership is unclear

Routine HR work runs late.

Joining formalities, letters and confirmations wait until someone has time.

Documentation is inconsistent.

Each joiner's file looks different, and gaps show up at audit or exit.

Records are fragmented.

Employee data sits across the HRMS, spreadsheets, finance and email.

Follow-ups get missed.

Probation reviews, leave balances and exit clearances slip through.

HR sits with the founder, finance or admin.

People who have other jobs are running HR in the gaps.

Nobody can say what's pending.

Leadership has no simple view of HR workload, backlog or issues.

The service

What HR outsourcing means at PathWeave

HR outsourcing at PathWeave is an ongoing managed service for a defined set of HR processes. Before we start, we agree in writing:

ScopeWhich processes PathWeave runs, and which it doesn't.
Operating modelWho does what, through which channels and systems.
Service measuresTimeliness, accuracy and backlog targets for each process.
GovernanceA named PathWeave owner, a named client owner and a quarterly review.
Retained responsibilitiesThe decisions and sign-offs that stay with your organisation.
ExclusionsAnything outside the agreed scope, which is handled as a change request.

You don't have to outsource everything. Some clients hand over one process group; others hand over most of HR operations while keeping HR leadership in-house or with a fractional HR leader.

What we run

HR processes we can manage

01 Core

Employee lifecycle administration

Joining, documentation, confirmation, transfers and role changes, leave administration and exits — from offer acceptance to final clearance.

02 Core

Payroll and statutory compliance

Monthly payroll processing, statutory deductions and filings, and the compliance calendar that goes with them. PathWeave prepares and files; your organisation approves payroll and signs off as the employer.

03 Core

HR records, data and reporting

Employee master data, document control, recurring HR reports and a people dashboard. Where you need a new system, see HR operations and HRMS support.

04 Optional

Recruitment

Sourcing, screening, interview scheduling, candidate communication, offer coordination and onboarding handover for your open roles. For hiring-process design, see recruitment and talent consulting.

05 Optional

Performance-cycle administration

Performance calendar, forms and workflow tracking, reminders, completion reporting and support for managers during review cycles.

06 Optional

HR helpdesk and manager support

Support for employee and manager HR queries through email, WhatsApp and on-site days, in the mix your organisation needs. Channels, availability and escalation for sensitive issues are agreed before the service starts and adjusted whenever the need changes.

PathWeave ownerA named service lead accountable for delivery and escalation.
WorkflowsDocumented procedures for every process in scope.

Accountability

What stays with your organisation

Outsourcing moves the work, not the accountability for people decisions. These stay with you:

Leadership decisionsHiring approvals, organisation changes, compensation and promotions.
Statutory sign-offThe employer's legal and compliance responsibilities.
Sensitive employee decisionsDisciplinary action, terminations and grievance outcomes.
Other agreed accountabilitiesRecorded in the responsibility matrix at the start.

PathWeave prepares, coordinates and documents; your named owners decide and sign.
