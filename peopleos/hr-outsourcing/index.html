WHEN HR BECOMES TOO MUCH TO MANAGE INTERNALLY

HR isn't a collection of disconnected tasks.

As organisations grow, HR responsibilities multiply.

Recruitment needs to keep pace with growth. Employees need consistent onboarding and support. Policies need to be maintained. HR records need to stay organised. Managers need support with people matters. Performance processes need to happen. HR systems need to work. Leadership needs reliable people information.

And someone needs to keep all of these activities connected.

For some organisations, building a large internal HR team for every requirement doesn't make sense.

The challenge becomes: How do you build a reliable HR function without having to build every capability yourself?

EXTENDING YOUR HR TEAM

Outsourcing doesn't have to mean replacing your HR team.

An organisation may already have an HR manager, HR executives, founder-led HR, finance or administration teams handling HR, or specialist HR capability in some areas. PathWeave can complement those capabilities.

What should stay internal?

Retain responsibilities where internal ownership creates the most value, partnering seamlessly with your internal team or fractional HR leaders to deliver results.

What should be supported externally?

Identify activities where additional operating capacity, specialist capability or consistent execution would help.

Where does specialist capability add value?

Bring in focused expertise where the organisation does not need to build a permanent internal capability.

How should responsibilities connect?

Define practical handoffs and ownership so internal and external teams operate as one connected HR function.
