PeopleOS · People & HR transformation

People and HR systems that hold up as you grow.

PeopleOS helps organisations build the structures, roles, manager routines, HR processes and technology they need so people decisions stop depending on a few individuals. For growing businesses, our flagship focus is HR Consulting for SMEs.

Explore HR for SMEsStart with an HR Health Check

Usually the moment to call

  • Founders still handle routine people decisions
  • Managers apply policies or performance standards differently
  • Roles and decision ownership are unclear
  • Hiring is scaling faster than onboarding and manager capability
  • Employee data and workflows are fragmented
  • The HR team is busy but the operating model is still unclear

Our approach

HR is part of the operating system of the business.

As organisations grow, people decisions become business decisions. Structures, roles, capabilities, processes and HR systems need to evolve with the organisation.

The focus is not HR policy or administration for its own sake. It is the people infrastructure that lets a growing business run with clearer ownership, stronger managers and less dependence on individual memory or founder intervention.
