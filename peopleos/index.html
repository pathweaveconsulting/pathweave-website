People and HR systems that hold up as you grow.

PeopleOS is PathWeave’s People & HR Transformation practice. We help organisations build the structures, roles, processes and HR technology they need — so people decisions stop depending on a few individuals.

Our approach

HR is part of the operating system of the business.

As organisations grow, people decisions become business decisions. Structures, roles, capabilities, processes and HR systems need to evolve with the organisation.

The focus is not HR policy or administration for its own sake. It is the people infrastructure that lets a growing business run without the founder in every decision.
