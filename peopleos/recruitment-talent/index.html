WHEN HIRING BECOMES A BOTTLENECK

Finding people is difficult. A weak hiring process makes it harder.

For growing organisations, the challenge isn't just identifying candidates. It's often the process of evaluating, managing and successfully onboarding them.

Hiring managers may lack clarity on what the role actually requires. Interviews may lack structure, leading to inconsistent decisions. Communication with candidates may be slow or disorganised. And once a candidate is selected, poor onboarding can undermine the entire effort.

PathWeave helps organisations build a more reliable, structured and effective approach to recruitment and talent.

WHERE RECRUITMENT TYPICALLY BREAKS DOWN

Is your hiring process working against you?

Unclear Role Requirements

Hiring starts before the business has fully defined what the role requires and how success will be measured.

Inconsistent Interviews

Interviewers ask different questions, making it difficult to objectively compare candidates.

Slow Decision Making

A disorganised process extends the hiring timeline, causing the organisation to lose good candidates.

Poor Candidate Experience

Lack of communication or a confusing interview process damages the employer brand.

Ineffective Onboarding

New hires are brought in but aren't given the structure or support they need to become productive quickly.

Lack of Hiring Data

The organisation cannot track its hiring metrics, making it hard to improve the process.

IS THIS THE RIGHT SUPPORT FOR YOU?

You may need Recruitment & Talent support if…

Your hiring process is slow or disorganised. You are losing good candidates because decisions take too long or communication is poor.
Interviews are inconsistent. Different managers evaluate candidates differently, leading to unpredictable hiring outcomes.
New hires are struggling to integrate. Your onboarding process lacks structure, delaying time to productivity or increasing early attrition.
Roles aren't clearly defined. You find yourself hiring for a title without a clear understanding of the specific responsibilities and required skills.
Your ATS isn't helping. You are either managing recruitment on spreadsheets or using an ATS that doesn't fit your actual process.
You lack visibility into hiring metrics. You cannot easily track time-to-hire, source quality or recruitment bottlenecks.
