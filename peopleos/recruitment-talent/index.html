Where hiring breaks down

Hiring problems often begin before the interview

The role isn't defined.

Hiring starts before anyone agrees what the role must achieve in its first year.

Requirements change mid-process.

The brief shifts after interviews begin, and good candidates drop out.

Interviews are inconsistent.

Each interviewer asks different questions, so candidates can't be compared fairly.

Decisions are slow.

Feedback waits for days and offers go out after the candidate has accepted elsewhere.

Onboarding is an afterthought.

New hires join without a plan, and early attrition undoes the hiring effort.

Nobody measures hiring.

Time-to-hire, conversion and quality of hire aren't tracked, so the process never improves.

What we do

Recruitment and talent consulting services

We design the hiring system, help you implement it on live roles, and train hiring managers to run it.

01

Role and success profile design

Role outcomes, responsibilities, capabilities and first-year expectations, agreed before the role is advertised.

02

Recruitment workflow design

Requisition and approvals, hiring stages, ownership, candidate communication and decision timing, plus guidance on selecting or configuring an ATS that fits the process.

03

Structured interviews and assessment

Interview plans, question banks, scorecards, work samples and decision rules, so hiring decisions rest on evidence rather than impressions.

04

Candidate experience and communication

Consistent touchpoints, response times and expectation-setting from application to offer, including how the organisation presents itself to candidates.

05

Offer and onboarding connection

A defined path from offer acceptance through pre-joining to the first 90 days, so selected candidates arrive ready to contribute.

06

Recruitment metrics and review

Time-to-hire, stage conversion, source effectiveness, quality of hire and early attrition, reviewed on a regular rhythm.

Our approach

Process first, tools second

Define→Structure→Assess→Integrate

We define the roles, stages and assessment first, then choose or configure the ATS to support them, and integrate it with your wider HR operations and HRMS.

Process

How the engagement works

Diagnose→Redesign→Pilot→Enable→Measure
  1. 1. DiagnoseReview how you define roles, assess candidates and make decisions today, using recent hires as evidence.
  2. 2. RedesignBuild the role profiles, workflow, interview kits and scorecards.
  3. 3. PilotRun the new process on one or two live roles and fix what doesn't work.
  4. 4. EnableTrain hiring managers and interviewers and hand over the templates.
  5. 5. MeasureTrack hiring metrics and review them with your team to keep improving.
