PeopleOS · HR Transformation

Transform HR into a people operating system the business can actually run.

PathWeave helps growing organisations redesign the connected system behind HR: roles, processes, governance, HR technology, data, manager routines and service delivery. The goal is an HR function that is less dependent on individuals and more capable of supporting the next stage of growth.

Discuss HR transformationStart with an HR Health Check

Typical signs

  • HR work depends on a few experienced individuals
  • Policies exist but are applied differently across teams
  • Employee data lives across spreadsheets and disconnected systems
  • Managers escalate routine people decisions to HR or founders
  • HR spends most of its time administering rather than improving
  • The organisation has outgrown the way HR was originally set up

What transformation means

Redesign the system, not just the HR team.

HR transformation is broader than implementing an HRMS or rewriting policies. It asks how people work should be organised across HR, managers and employees, which processes need standardisation, what technology should support them, and how decisions should be governed.

The work starts with the operating model and employee lifecycle. Technology follows the business design rather than defining it.

Transformation layers

What may need to change together

01

HR operating model

Roles, responsibilities, service boundaries, decision rights and how work moves between HR, managers and employees.

02

Employee lifecycle processes

Recruitment, joining, changes, performance, development and exits designed as consistent end-to-end workflows.

03

Policies and governance

Practical policy architecture, approval rules, ownership and escalation so the organisation applies people decisions consistently.

04

HR technology and data

HRMS requirements, employee records, workflow automation, reporting and data ownership that support the operating model.

05

Manager capability

Manager routines and decision support for hiring, feedback, performance, employee issues and team operations.

06

Adoption and improvement

Role-based enablement, transition support and measures that show whether the new HR system is actually being used.
