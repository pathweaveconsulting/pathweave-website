WHY FRACTIONAL HR

You may need more HR capability. You may not need a full-time hire.

As a business grows, HR requirements rarely arrive in a neat sequence. One month the priority may be hiring. The next may be policies, performance, employee relations, HR operations or a new HRMS.

A fractional model gives you access to experienced HR capability around the requirements that matter now, without forcing the organisation into a full-time structure before it is ready.

PathWeave can work alongside founders, leadership teams and existing HR resources, taking ownership of defined requirements or providing broader ongoing support.

WHEN IT MAKES SENSE

When is fractional HR the right choice?

The right time to consider fractional HR is not determined by employee count alone. It is often when the complexity of the organisation has started to outgrow the HR capability available to it.

Your HR requirements have outgrown available capacity.

There is more to manage, but not enough need yet for another full-time senior role.

The founder or leadership team is still carrying HR.

People issues and processes are consuming time that should be spent on the business.

You are growing faster than your HR systems.

Hiring, onboarding, policies, performance or employee information are becoming inconsistent.

You need experienced support during a transition.

Expansion, restructuring, rapid growth or a change in HR leadership creates a temporary capability gap.
