Manufacturing Advisory

Plant Profit Improvement for Manufacturers in Delhi NCR

For auto-component, electronics, packaging and engineering plants across NCR that are squeezed between OEM price-down demands and rising input costs.

Part of Manufacturing Advisory - Plant Profit for Manufacturers.

Where we work

We work with plants across Delhi NCR and its neighbouring industrial belts in Haryana and Uttar Pradesh.

The NCR margin squeeze

Supplier plants often sell to OEMs on annual contracts with built-in price-downs, while steel, polymer, copper and packaging costs move monthly. Three issues come up again and again:

  • Contract and price-variation terms that recover input costs slowly or not at all.
  • Multi-shift operations where contract labour and overtime have grown faster than output.
  • Changeover and downtime losses from high-mix, low-batch production.

How we deliver in NCR

Planning, analysis and reporting run remotely. Shopfloor work is on-site, in scheduled visit blocks.

What we offer Delhi NCR plants

  • Plant Profit Diagnostic: 45 days across every cost area.
  • 30-day sprints on a single area, such as manpower, material, purchase prices, rejection, energy or price recovery. See all 13 sprints.
  • Implementation support: about six months, with a monthly Steering Committee.
