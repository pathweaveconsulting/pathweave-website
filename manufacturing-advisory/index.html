Manufacturing Advisory

Plant Profit for Manufacturers

Your plant's profit is decided on the shopfloor, not in the boardroom. PathWeave's Manufacturing Advisory practice finds it there, puts a number on it in rupees, and stays until it shows up in your P&L.

Start with the 45-day Plant Profit Diagnostic or pick a focused 30-day sprint below.

Who this is for

  • Founder-led and promoter-run manufacturers, with one plant or several.
  • Plants whose sales are growing while margins are falling.
  • Plant heads and CFOs under pressure from rising input costs, wage inflation or customer price-downs.
  • Businesses that have tried cost-cutting before, but the savings did not last.

Two ways to start

The flagship: Plant Profit Diagnostic (45 days)

A full review across every cost area. Every leakage is sized in rupees per month, prioritised, agreed with your CFO and turned into a six-month implementation plan.

How savings are counted

  • Baseline: the average of the three months before work starts, as a percentage of sales.
  • Commodity prices excluded: raw-material price swings are taken out, so only real improvement counts.
  • Realised, not promised: a saving counts only after it has held for three consecutive months.
  • Signed off by your CFO: your finance team agrees every number.

Frequently asked questions

What is plant profitability consulting?
Structured work that raises a manufacturing plant's EBITDA by reducing controllable costs, such as material, manpower, rejection, energy and overheads, and by recovering money owed or lost commercially. Each saving is tracked until it appears in the P&L.
Should we start with the diagnostic or a sprint?
If margins are falling and the causes are unclear, start with the diagnostic. If you already know the problem area, a 30-day sprint is faster and smaller.
Do you replace our plant team?
No. Your team owns the actions. We diagnose, plan, run governance and work alongside your managers until the changes hold.
Which industries do you work with?
Process and discrete manufacturing, including plastics and polymers, engineering, auto components, chemicals, pharma, packaging and food processing.
Do you publish fees?
Every diagnostic and sprint is a fixed fee, agreed upfront after a short conversation about your plant's size and scope.
