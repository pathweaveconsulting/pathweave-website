Manufacturing Advisory

Price Recovery and Product Margin Review

Input prices move every month; customer prices move once a year, if at all. In between, the plant absorbs the difference, and many businesses never measure how much they have lost, or which parts actually lose money.

Part of Manufacturing Advisory - Plant Profit for Manufacturers. A fixed-fee, 30-day sprint.

Is this for you?

This sprint fits if any of these sound familiar:

  • Raw-material increases (resin, steel, copper, chemicals) were absorbed rather than passed on.
  • Some parts may be loss-making without anyone knowing.
  • Quoted margins are not tracked against actual margins.
  • Engineering changes or tooling have not been billed.

What you get in 30 days

  1. Part-wise margin for your top parts.
  2. Commodity recovery gap by customer.
  3. Quoted vs actual margin, with should-cost models for RFQs.
  4. Engineering changes and tooling not yet recovered.
  5. A customer-wise renegotiation pack, including price-variation terms.

How the 30 days run

WeekFocus
1Kick-off with the sponsor, data request, first plant walk

What we need from you

  • Sales register and price history
  • Raw-material price movement
  • BOM and cost sheets
  • RFQ cost sheets for top parts
  • Engineering change register

Who we meet: Sales head, Costing and RFQ, CFO, Purchase.

After the sprint

You keep every finding. If you choose to continue, the natural next step is commercial recovery support for your sales team. We can also run the full Plant Profit Diagnostic to look across every cost area, or move straight to implementation support.

Frequently asked questions

Will this damage customer relationships?
Evidence-based, well-timed revisions protect relationships better than sudden large increases. Your sales team stays the face to the customer.
Do you talk to our customers directly?
No. We work behind your sales team; they lead every customer conversation.
What is a price-variation clause?
A contract term that adjusts the selling price when a named input cost, such as a published resin or metal index, moves beyond an agreed band, so future increases are recovered automatically.
Is the sprint paid?
Yes. It is a fixed fee agreed upfront, and the findings are yours whether or not you continue.
