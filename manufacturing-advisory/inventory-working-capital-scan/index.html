Manufacturing Advisory

Inventory and Working Capital Scan

Many plants run short of the items they need while warehouses hold stock nobody has touched in a year. Add stretched receivables, and cash gets locked up even when the P&L looks fine.

Part of Manufacturing Advisory - Plant Profit for Manufacturers. A fixed-fee, 30-day sprint.

Is this for you?

This sprint fits if any of these sound familiar:

  • Stock-outs and excess stock happen at the same time.
  • Dead and slow-moving stock has built up.
  • Receivables are running beyond agreed terms.
  • Safety stock norms have not been reset in years.

What you get in 30 days

  1. Items below safety stock, and dead and slow-moving stock.
  2. Reset safety stock and min-max norms.
  3. Spares stock review.
  4. Payables and receivables days compared with terms.
  5. An estimate of the cash that can be released.

How the 30 days run

WeekFocus
1Kick-off with the sponsor, data request, first plant walk
2Analysis and shopfloor checks
3Findings tested with your team and sized in rupees
4Read-out to leadership, with a pilot plan and next step

What we need from you

  • Stock statements with norms
  • Consumption by item
  • Non-moving stock list
  • Spares stock
  • Creditors and debtors ageing

Who we meet: Stores, PPC, Purchase, CFO, Maintenance.

After the sprint

You keep every finding. If you choose to continue, the natural next step is a stores and planning discipline programme. We can also run the full Plant Profit Diagnostic to look across every cost area, or move straight to implementation support.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a P&L or a cash saving?
Mainly cash. Lower inventory and faster collections release working capital; carrying cost and write-off savings also show up in the P&L.
What happens to dead stock?
We classify it into use, return, sell or write off, and give a disposal plan for your finance team to approve.
Is the sprint paid?
Yes. It is a fixed fee agreed upfront, and the findings are yours whether or not you continue.
