Manufacturing Advisory

Implementation Support: Turning Findings into P&L Savings

Most cost programmes fail after the report is delivered. Implementation support is what turns findings into money in the P&L.

Part of Manufacturing Advisory - Plant Profit for Manufacturers. This covers the Design, Implement and Sustain stages, after a diagnostic or a sprint.

Design (2-4 weeks)

A workplan for each initiative, with actions, owners, milestones and monthly savings targets agreed with your CFO.

Implement (about 6 months)

  • Pilots on selected lines or departments before roll-out.
  • SOPs and manning norms so changes hold on every shift.
  • Activity tracker: every action with owner, dates, status and expected value.
  • Weekly progress update to the sponsor, with the tracker attached.
  • Monthly Steering Committee with top management: shopfloor observations, insights, savings against target, decisions needed, and a named owner for every action.
  • Shopfloor presence throughout, working with supervisors.

Sustain (ongoing)

Control registers and a handover to your plant team, followed by quarterly health checks. When you are ready to extend to your next plant, a shorter diagnostic gets it started.

How savings are counted

  • Baseline: the average of the three months before work starts, as a percentage of sales.
  • Commodity prices excluded: raw-material price swings are taken out, so only real improvement counts.
  • Realised, not promised: a saving counts only after it has held for three consecutive months.
  • Signed off by your CFO: your finance team agrees every number.

Frequently asked questions

Can you run implementation for a plan you didn't prepare?
Yes. If you already have a cost-reduction plan, we can set up and run its governance.
Who attends the Steering Committee?
The promoter or CEO, the plant head, finance and the action owners for the period under review.
What does the plant team keep at the end?
The tracker, templates, SOPs and review rhythm, ready to run without us.
