Manufacturing Advisory

Incentives and Recoveries Review

Money your plant is already entitled to often goes unclaimed: state investment incentives, GST credits, disputable customer debit notes and engineering changes that were never billed.

Part of Manufacturing Advisory - Plant Profit for Manufacturers. A fixed-fee, 30-day sprint.

Is this for you?

This sprint fits if any of these sound familiar:

  • Your plant has invested in capacity in recent years.
  • You export or supply under government schemes.
  • Customer debit notes are accepted without review.
  • Engineering changes or tooling were never billed to customers.

What you get in 30 days

  1. Unclaimed state and central incentives.
  2. GST credit leakage and refunds to pursue.
  3. Customer debit notes that can be disputed.
  4. Engineering changes and tooling not yet billed.
  5. A claims plan with the rupee value of each item.

How the 30 days run

WeekFocus
1Kick-off with the sponsor, data request, first plant walk
2Analysis and shopfloor checks
3Findings tested with your team and sized in rupees
4Read-out to leadership, with a pilot plan and next step

What we need from you

  • Investment and capex records
  • Incentive claims already filed
  • GST returns reconciliation
  • Customer debit notes
  • Engineering change register

Who we meet: CFO, Tax and accounts, Sales, Plant head.

After the sprint

You keep every finding. If you choose to continue, the natural next step is claims filing and recovery support, together with PathWeave's Government Advisory practice. We can also run the full Plant Profit Diagnostic to look across every cost area, or move straight to implementation support.

Frequently asked questions

Do you file GST returns or tax claims?
No. We find and size the opportunities. Statutory filings stay with your chartered accountant or tax advisor, and we work alongside them.
Which incentives do you look at?
Incentives under your state's industrial policy and relevant central schemes, depending on your investments, location and products.
Is the sprint paid?
Yes. It is a fixed fee agreed upfront, and the findings are yours whether or not you continue.
