Manufacturing Advisory

Plant Profit Improvement for Manufacturers in Hyderabad

Hyderabad is PathWeave's home base, so shopfloor work here runs with regular, close-at-hand presence.

Part of Manufacturing Advisory - Plant Profit for Manufacturers.

Where we work

We work with plants in and around Hyderabad and across Telangana.

What we see in Hyderabad plants

The region's mix of bulk-drug and formulation units, plastics and packaging converters, engineering shops and food processors shares common pressures:

  • High contract-labour dependence and frequent shift overtime.
  • Utility-heavy processes where power, steam and compressed air drive conversion cost.
  • Input prices (solvents, APIs, polymers, steel) that move faster than customer prices.

What we offer Hyderabad plants

  • Plant Profit Diagnostic: 45 days across every cost area.
  • 30-day sprints on a single area, such as manpower, material, purchase prices, rejection, energy or price recovery. See all 13 sprints.
  • Implementation support: about six months, with a monthly Steering Committee.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with pharma plants?
Yes, on the operating cost side: manpower, utilities, consumables, yield and overtime. GMP and quality-system changes stay with your QA team.
Do you handle Telangana labour-law aspects of manpower changes?
Yes. Redeployment and contract-labour changes are planned with our PeopleOS statutory team.
Can we start with one department?
Yes. A sprint can start with your largest-cost area and extend once you have seen the method.
