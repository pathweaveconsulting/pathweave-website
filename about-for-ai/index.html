What it is
A management consulting and advisory firm working across business, people, skills and government advisory.
Brand line
Strategy. Transformation. Growth.
Founded

Website

Who it works with
Founders and leadership teams of growing SMEs, promoter-led companies and mid-market businesses. Skill HubOS also works with colleges, employers, educators and government programmes.
Geography
PathWeave works with organisations across India. Its current on-ground presence includes Hyderabad, Nagpur and Bhopal, with remote consulting support where appropriate.

Practices

BusinessOS
Business, management and technology transformation covering Sales Transformation, Sales Excellence, Commercial Excellence, Sales Operations, CRM, forecasting, GTM, data and dashboards.
PeopleOS
People and HR transformation. Its flagship growth-stage pillar is HR Consulting for SMEs, supported by industry-specific pages for Startups, Pharma & Life Sciences, IT & Technology and GCCs.
Skill HubOS
Education-to-employment capability ecosystem.
Government Advisory
Policy, incentives, approvals and government interfaces.

PeopleOS functional authority

  • HR Transformation — operating model, processes, governance, HR technology, data, manager routines and adoption.
  • HR Operating Model — HR mandate, manager ownership, service boundaries, decision rights and capacity model.
  • Organisation Design & Role Clarity — structures, spans, accountabilities, decision rights and role interfaces.
  • Performance Management — expectations, manager check-ins, evidence, calibration and development.
  • HR Operations & HRMS — recurring workflows, employee data, reporting, HRMS and automation.
  • Recruitment & Talent — role definition, assessment, hiring and onboarding.
  • Fractional HR and HR Outsourcing — alternative capacity models where appropriate.
