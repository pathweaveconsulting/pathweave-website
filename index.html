Questions

Before you get in touch

What does PathWeave actually do?

PathWeave is a management consulting firm. We help founders and leadership teams fix the systems behind growth: sales processes, CRM, dashboards, HR and people processes, roles and the routines through which work gets reviewed. We diagnose the problem, design a practical answer and, where it helps, work with your team to put it in place.

Who does PathWeave work with?

Mostly growing SMEs, promoter-led companies and mid-market businesses that are moving from founder-led decisions to structured management. Our primary market is Hyderabad and Secunderabad, with local offices in Nagpur and Bhopal and remote consulting support for Indore and Delhi NCR.

What is the difference between a Health Check and a consulting engagement?

A Health Check reviews one agreed area at a small fixed price agreed upfront. You receive a gap map, prioritised fixes, a recommended next step and, if more support would help, a defined scope. A consulting engagement is scoped separately and usually covers design and implementation across a wider set of problems.

Do you only advise, or do you also help implement?

Both. Where it is needed, our work extends into implementation: processes, governance, CRM and reporting set-up, HR operations, capability building and the operating routines that make the change stick. You decide how much of that you want us to carry.

Can an organisation start with only one problem?

Yes, and we usually recommend it. The HR Health Check and the Sales & CRM Health Check are designed for exactly that: one area, one agreed question, and findings you can use whether or not you continue with PathWeave.

How is company information handled?

Before work begins, we agree what information the review actually needs, and we work only with what you choose to share. If you need a non-disclosure agreement in place before sharing details, raise it in the first conversation.

What happens after the first conversation?

We take time to understand the context before suggesting anything. If there is a sensible next step, we agree the question, the information required, the scope and the price before any work begins. Sometimes the right answer is a guide or an internal fix, and we will say so.

Tell us what you’re trying to solve.

A short conversation is usually enough to tell whether a guide, a focused review or a wider engagement makes sense.
